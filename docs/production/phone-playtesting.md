# Brackenford on your phone

1. Open [Releases](https://github.com/Jamie1171/Jrpg/releases) and choose the newest
   **Brackenford opening** preview.
2. Download `UnfinishedDawn-0.1.0.apk`, open the download and follow your phone's
   installation prompts. This is a directly installed development preview, not a
   Play Store listing. Android's installation requirements depend on your device.
3. Open **Unfinished Dawn**, hold the phone sideways, and choose **Begin your journey**.

No game accounts, API keys, subscriptions or network connection are needed to play.
The APK targets ARM64 Android phones, including the Pixel 9a. Physical-device
testing is still needed; a successful export does not prove performance on a phone.

## Playing the opening

Tap open ground to walk. Tap a person or a circular marker to walk over and interact,
or use the interaction button at the bottom right when nearby. The gold diamond
marks the next story objective, including the exit needed to reach another location.

Read conversations with **Continue**. **Skip scene** preserves the conversation in
the journal and applies the same story progress. No choices depend on hearing audio.

The opening route is: Mira's parcel → Orren at the landing → Cael at the northern
path → the woodland cart → Mira at the inn → Ysra. Exploration and fishing can take
as long as you like. Mining and crafting are not playable in this build.

In battle, select a foe with its name button. Rowan's Opening Cut exposes it to two
stronger hits. Cael's Shield Bash interrupts its next turn. The top banner tells you
when a charge is coming; guard to reduce damage and recover focus. Herbs heal the
most injured party member and can revive a fallen ally. Defeat offers retry or retreat.

At the landing, choose a fishing spot. Guide the rod when the fish turns, reel when
it rests, and give line when it surges. Catch three fish to earn the riverglass charm
(+4 maximum health for Rowan). Sell spare fish by visiting Petra in the square and
opening the satchel. These rewards are separate from the main story.

## Saving and useful feedback

The game saves locally at story checkpoints and while exploring. **Menu → Save &
title** is the clearest way to finish a session. Closing the app during a battle
returns to the checkpoint before that battle; unfinished casts are not saved.
Uninstalling the app removes its save. Ordinary APK updates use the same preview
identity and are intended to retain it. There is no cloud save yet.

If movement gets awkward, **Menu → Return to the village** moves you to safe ground.
You can turn music off, reduce walking animation and enlarge dialogue in Settings.

For the first phone playtest, please check:

- Can you read the dialogue and easily tap the buttons without zooming?
- Can you walk to the landing and reach the woodland cart?
- Is the battle's charge warning clear, and does victory feel earned?
- Does fishing feel like a small skill to learn?
- Does **Save & title → Continue** return you to the correct objective?
- After briefly switching to another app, does the game resume correctly?

For a problem, report the location, what you tapped, what happened and the build
number from the release. A screenshot is especially useful. No need to install
Godot, Blender or any development tools on the phone.
